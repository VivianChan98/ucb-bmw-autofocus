About those turkeys
15
not one is from here.
Wild turkeys are not native to the Bay Area. The Fish & Game Commission introduced them across California between 1959 and 1999 for hunting. They adapted to mild Bay winters, spread into parks and greenbelts, and never left. The flock at César Chávez has been here since the early 2000s.
The ground beneath you
90
acres of park on top of a landfill.
Berkeley used this area as a dump from 1957 to the late 1980s. In 1991 the site was sealed, graded, and landscaped into César Chávez Park. The kite hill where those turkeys were standing is built on thirty years of garbage.
